**Full description:**

Deckd is a card table that fits in your pocket. No accounts, no setup, no waiting for everyone to download anything. One phone, one deck, everyone plays.

PASS & PLAY
Deal a hand, flip cards, pass the phone. The privacy veil keeps every hand secret until it's your turn. Perfect for a pub table, a sofa, or a long train ride.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS ANYWHERE
A Deckd Master hosts a lobby and gets a room code. Friends join free from their own phones, wherever they are. Turn-based play over a fast relay, no accounts needed.

MASTER THE TABLE
Deal, Draw, Shuffle, or Master passes unlock hosting. Guests always play free. One Master brings the whole table.

YOUR TABLE, YOUR RULES
Pick a preset or just deal it your way. Freeplay, deal two each, blackjack-style, poker-style. Custom card backs and table felts.

- 2-6 players pass & play on one phone
- Multiplayer lobbies with join codes
- Privacy veil pass ritual
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking
- Works offline for pass & play

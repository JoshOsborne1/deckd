Host mode advertises a Deckd service on-device. Guests scan and connect — no router, no account. iOS and Android may ask for Bluetooth permission; sessions are foreground-first.
OPTIONAL
Network multiplayer
When enabled, future builds can use LAN or a relay on top of the same event protocol. Does not replace BLE.
